05Account tiers and verification

Poket uses a tiered verification model. Each tier requires additional identity information and unlocks additional services and transaction limits:

  • Tier 0 (Registered): Email, phone, and self-attested age. Limited monetary activity.
  • Tier 1 (Basic): Government ID and liveness check (via our identity verification partner), plus sanctions and watchlist screening. Unlocks deposits, withdrawals, wallet, and peer-to-peer transfers.
  • Tier 2 (Standard): Proof of address, in addition to Tier 1. Unlocks higher transaction limits.
  • Tier 3 (Enhanced): Source of Funds declaration and additional document validation, in addition to Tier 2. Unlocks virtual account details, wire transfers, virtual cards, and the highest transaction limits.

Transaction limits at each tier are set by us and may change. Current limits are available in the app.

We screen you against sanctions and watchlists at verification and monthly thereafter. If you are flagged as a sanctions match, your account will be restricted and, where required, reported to regulators.
